The prompts from the video
1. Context (the new-coworker test):
   I sell handmade soy candles on Etsy. Write a product description for my lavender candle. It burns for 40 hours and comes in a reusable glass jar. Most buyers get it as a gift.
   Role (optional): You are an experienced copywriter for small online shops.
2. Format:
   Under 80 words. Warm, calm tone. Format: a title line, two short sentences, then three bullet points.
   Comparing things? "...in a table with columns: price, battery life, weight."
   Say what to DO, not what to avoid: "Use words a 12-year-old knows" / "Three sentences maximum" / "Start with a surprising fact".
3. Example (few-shot):
   Here is a description I wrote that I love: "Cedar & Smoke. Like a campfire, without the smoke in your hair. 45 hours of slow, even burn." Write the lavender candle in the same style and length.
   Tip: wrap pasted text in quotes or in simple tags.
4. Steps: list 10 name ideas, pick one, write the description, write 3 listing titles. One prompt per step.
   Math or logic: "Work through it step by step, then give the final answer."
5. Questions: Before you start, ask me up to 3 questions about anything you need to know.

Reusable template:
I am a [who you are]. Write a [what] for [who it's for]. Format: [length], [tone], [structure]. Match this example: "[paste it]". Before you start, ask me up to 3 questions.

Remember: AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Check numbers, dates, names and quotes, and never paste passwords or private data.
The AI replies shown in the video are illustrations; real replies vary every time. Shift + Enter adds a new line without sending in most chat apps.
