The formulas from the video (practice table in A1:E13: Order, Rep, Region, Product, Amount)
1. XLOOKUP:   =XLOOKUP(G2,A2:A13,E2:E13,"Not found")
   whole row: =XLOOKUP(G2,A2:A13,B2:E13,"Not found")
2. FILTER:    =FILTER(A2:E13,C2:C13="West")
   two conditions (AND): =FILTER(A2:E13,(C2:C13="West")*(E2:E13＞100))
   with a message: =FILTER(A2:E13,C2:C13="Central","No orders")
3. UNIQUE:    =UNIQUE(B2:B13)
4. SORT:      =SORT(A2:E13,5,-1)     and   =SORT(UNIQUE(C2:C13))
5. SUMIFS:    =SUMIFS(E2:E13,B2:B13,"Ana")
   two conditions: =SUMIFS(E2:E13,C2:C13,"West",D2:D13,"Laptop")
   Bonus report: G2 =UNIQUE(B2:B13)  then  H2 =SUMIFS(E2:E13,B2:B13,G2#)
6. IFS:       =IFS(E2≥1000,"Large",E2≥100,"Medium",TRUE,"Small")
7. TEXTSPLIT: =TEXTSPLIT(A2," ")     down a column: =TEXTSPLIT(A2,,";")
(YouTube blocks the greater-than sign in descriptions: retype ＞ as greater-than and ≥ as greater-than + equals in Excel.)

Works in Excel for Microsoft 365, Excel 2021 and Excel 2024. TEXTSPLIT needs Microsoft 365 or Excel 2024. #SPILL! = something is in the way of the results. #CALC! = FILTER found nothing and has no 3rd argument.
