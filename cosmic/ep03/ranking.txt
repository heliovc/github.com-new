TITLE (Short):
Top 5 Fastest Things Ever Measured ⚡ #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
From Voyager 1 to a single proton that nearly tied with light. #1 would lose a race against light across the whole Milky Way by about 4 millimeters.

5. Voyager 1 — about 61,000 km/h (17 km/s)
4. Parker Solar Probe — about 692,000 km/h (430,000 mph), fastest human-made object
3. S5-HVS1 — about 1,755 km/s, a star ejected from the Milky Way by its central black hole
2. S4714 — about 24,000 km/s, 8% of the speed of light
1. Oh-My-God particle — 99.99999999999999999999957% of light speed (1991)

Sources: NASA Science, Voyager 1: What Is a Light-Day (cruising at nearly 38,000 mph / 61,000 km/h); NASA Science, Parker Solar Probe Reports Successful Closest Approach to Sun (Dec 24, 2024: about 430,000 mph); Koposov et al. (2019), The Great Escape: discovery of a nearby 1700 km/s star ejected from the Milky Way by Sgr A* (MNRAS); Peissker et al. (2020), Astrophysical Journal: S4714 at about 24,000 km/s (ESO Very Large Telescope data); Scientific American, Where did the 'Oh-My-God' particle come from? (Fly's Eye, 1991, ~3.2×10^20 eV); Own calculation: a 3.2×10^20 eV proton (Lorentz factor ~3.4×10^11) loses to light by ~4 mm over 100,000 light-years.
