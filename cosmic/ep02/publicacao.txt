TITLE:
What If the Sun Disappeared Forever?

DESCRIPTION:
The Sun vanishes, and this time it never comes back. No light, no heat, no gravity. How long would Earth last, and what would happen first? We run the clock from the first 8 minutes to millions of years: the sky turns black at noon, the Moon goes dark, Earth flies off in a straight line, the oceans freeze over, and the air itself falls as snow. But deep down, life holds on.
