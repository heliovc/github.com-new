TITLE (Short):
Top 5 Largest Stars Ever Measured 🌟 #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
If these stars replaced our Sun, how far would they reach? #1 goes almost to Saturn's orbit.

5. R Doradus — about 350× wider than the Sun
4. Antares — about 700× wider than the Sun
3. Betelgeuse — outer atmosphere past Jupiter's orbit
2. VY Canis Majoris — 300,000× brighter than the Sun
1. WOH G64 — about 2,000× wider than the Sun

Star sizes are estimates; orbits drawn to scale.

Sources: ESO eso2412 (R Doradus, ALMA, 2024); ESO eso1726 (Antares, VLTI, 2017); NASA Science — Betelgeuse location and size (Hubble); NASA Hubble — "Hubble Solves Mystery of Monster Star's Dimming" (VY CMa, 2021); ESO eso2417 (WOH G64, 2024).
