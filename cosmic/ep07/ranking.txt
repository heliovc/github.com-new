Top 5 Longest Days in the Solar System ☀️ #shorts

DESCRIÇÃO
On one world, a single day lasts longer than two of its years. Counting down the 5 longest days (sunrise to sunrise) in our solar system.

Sources:
- Szakáts et al. (2023), Astronomy & Astrophysics 669, L3: Eris rotates in 15.8 days, tidally locked to its moon Dysnomia
- NASA Science, Earth's Moon: one lunar day lasts about 29.5 Earth days
- NASA / Cassini (via BBC Sky at Night): Iapetus orbits Saturn and rotates in 79 days; its leading side is as dark as a blackboard
- NASA Venus fact sheet: rotation 243 Earth days (retrograde); 116.75 days between sunrises
- NASA / BBC Sky at Night: Mercury rotates in about 59 days and orbits in 88; one solar day lasts 176 Earth days
